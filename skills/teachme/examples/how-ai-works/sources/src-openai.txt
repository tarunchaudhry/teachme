**What hallucinations are**
- "Hallucinations are plausible but false statements generated by language models."

**Why next-word pretraining leads to them**
- "Language models first learn through pretraining, a process of predicting the next word in huge amounts of text."
- "The model sees only positive examples of fluent language and must approximate the overall distribution."
- "But arbitrary low-frequency facts, like a pet’s birthday, cannot be predicted from patterns alone"

**Why grading that rewards guessing keeps them persisting**
- "Hallucinations persist partly because current evaluation methods set the wrong incentives."
- "when models are graded only on accuracy, the percentage of questions they get exactly right, they are encouraged to guess rather than say “I don’t know.”"
- "If the main scoreboards keep rewarding lucky guesses, models will keep learning to guess."
