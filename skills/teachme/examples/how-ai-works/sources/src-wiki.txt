**Tokenization and tokens**
- "As machine learning algorithms process numbers rather than text, the text must be converted to numbers."
- "Tokenization also compresses the datasets."

**Training by predicting the next token**
- "Generative pre-trained transformers (GPTs) are a type of LLM that is pre-trained to predict the next word."
- "Before being fine-tuned, most LLMs are next-token predictors."

**Parameters**
- "One float16 has 16 bits, or 2 bytes, and so one billion parameters require 2 gigabytes."

**Fine-tuning and reinforcement learning from human feedback**
- "Instruction fine-tuning is a form of supervised learning used to teach LLMs to follow user instructions."
- "RLHF involves training a reward model to predict which text humans prefer."
- "Then, the LLM can be fine-tuned through reinforcement learning to better satisfy this reward model."
